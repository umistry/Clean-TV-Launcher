# Wallpaper photo credits

The launcher ships each photo twice: a 1920x1080 `.jpg` that shows instantly, and a 3840x2160 HEVC
`.mp4` that shows at the panel's full 4K on the TV's video layer. `tools/wallpapers/still_to_hevc.swift`
makes the clips from the 4K originals, which stay outside the package in `wallpaper-sources/`.

The photos were downloaded from [Lorem Picsum](https://picsum.photos), which serves
photos from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com). Check the Unsplash License on each photo's page before
redistributing this launcher. Thank you to the photographers.
